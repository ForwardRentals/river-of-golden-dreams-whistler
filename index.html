The route

Alta Lake to Green Lake, the slow way.

The River of Golden Dreams winds out of Alta Lake, through wetland and willow, and into Green Lake under Wedge Mountain.

The paddle is easy. The hard part is that it only goes one way, so you need a boat at the top and a ride at the bottom. That's what the shuttle is for: we handle both ends.

  • Put-inRainbow Park, Alta Lake
  • TakeoutMeadow Park, near Green Lake
  • Time on the waterAbout 2–3 hours
  • Best seasonLate June through July, while the water is high

FAQ

Before you go.

Where does the River of Golden Dreams start and end?

The classic float starts at Rainbow Park on Alta Lake and ends at Meadow Park near Green Lake.

How long does it take?

About 2 to 3 hours, depending on water level and how often you stop.

Do I need my own canoe or a second car?

No. That is the whole point of the shuttle: the canoe is dropped at Rainbow Park and picked up at Meadow Park, so you don't need a vehicle at each end.

When is the best time to paddle it?

Late June through July, while the water is highest. Later in summer the river gets lower and slower.

How many people per canoe?

Up to three paddlers per canoe. Book more canoes for bigger groups.

Is it suitable for beginners?

It is a gentle float, but the river is narrow and winding, so some comfort steering a canoe helps. Ask when you book if you're unsure.

Rather stay on a lake?

The same crew delivers canoes to Alta, Lost, Green, Nita and Alpha lakes, plus Pemberton.
